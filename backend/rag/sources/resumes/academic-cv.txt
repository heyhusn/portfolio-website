RESEARCH EXPERIENCE
Lead Developer & First Author — VLVRAG: Vectorless Multimodal Video Retrieval, UMT	Mar 2026 – Present
Designed a vectorless retrieval architecture for lecture video: adaptive evidence units segmented on signal and transition cues, OCR of on-screen text fused with ASR speech on a common frame grid, and a three-level Markdown ledger (lecture → topic → timed unit) built by a summary-augmented tree in place of a vector database.
Retrieval unions deterministic timestamp resolution, two-stage tree traversal and BM25, reranked by a pedagogical evidence filter that scores cross-modal agreement and prioritises speech over slides where a lecturer corrects the displayed material.
Evaluated on reconstructed ground truth from MIT OpenCourseWare lectures. Against a count-matched equal-spacing baseline, segmentation error fell from Pk 0.512 to 0.344 and WindowDiff 0.513 to 0.391 ; on a second, denser course the system reached 92.0 NMI against 74.2 for an oracle-K baseline under a single pre-fitted configuration.
Solar PV Digital Twin — Applied ML for Solar Forecasting, UMT, 2026	solar-pv-digital-twin.vercel.app
Built an automated pipeline acquiring and processing 5.5+ years of hourly meteorological data from the NASA POWER API, with physics-based feature engineering (solar zenith angles, clear-sky irradiance models) and time-based interpolation for missing sensor records.
Trained and compared Random Forest, XGBoost and LSTM models for power-output forecasting across three climate regimes (Dhaka, Lahore, the Thar Desert), using SHAP for feature attribution and model interpretability.
Deployed the selected model behind a FastAPI inference service with an interactive 3D visualisation of the simulated facility, making the model’s behaviour inspectable rather than opaque.
